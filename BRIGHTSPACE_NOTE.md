**Three key insights**

1. **Growth opportunity: Electronics and the North.** Growth and Promising customers generate $12,024 (71% of $16,969 total revenue). Electronics alone brings in $5,340 of that, and Home & Furniture adds $2,270. By region and channel, North Online is the strongest cell ($2,480). Priority: concentrate marketing and inventory on Electronics and Home & Furniture, and push Online campaigns in the North to the Promising customers who are not yet in the Growth segment.

2. **Early warning of decline: unhappy customers and the Physical Store.** Decline customers average 1.58/5 satisfaction versus 4.0 to 4.45 for every other segment, and 17 of the 18 purchases rated 2 or lower are in Decline. They hold $3,650 (22% of revenue), of which 72% ($2,620) comes from Physical Stores and 43% ($1,570) from the West, with Electronics the largest lost category ($1,550). Satisfaction is the clearest leading indicator, so low ratings should trigger outreach before spending drops further.

3. **Strategic action: retain at-risk high-value buyers.** About $3,500 of revenue sits in purchases rated 2 or lower, and the largest are big-ticket items such as a $999.99 Electronics purchase rated 1. Recommendation: build a service-recovery and win-back program for West and East Physical Stores (follow-up calls, targeted offers, store service review), and protect Stable customers, who are the lowest-spend segment (avg $52 per purchase) and a candidate for cross-sell upsell.

*Caveat: 100 transactions over about five weeks, so these are directional.*
